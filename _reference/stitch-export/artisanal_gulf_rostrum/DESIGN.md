## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges modern Khaleeji coffee rituals with European specialty roasting refinement: quiet confidence, tactile warmth, curated hospitality, and architectural poise. The UI targets discerning locals, creative expatriates, and specialty coffee connoisseurs across the Emirates seeking an unhurried, elevated sensory experience.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Warmth** with structural **Tactile Minimalism**. It relies on generous negative space, purposeful typographic hierarchy, delicate hairline architectural rules, and warm ivory planes layered over cool, mineral undertones. The visual tone eschews generic cafe rusticism in favor of sharp, gallery-grade layouts suited for Dubai’s design-forward cafe landscape. The dual-script typographic system ensures English and Arabic (قهوة) sit in visual parity, honoring right-to-left layout dynamics with native fluidity.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#12303A` - Deep Mineral Teal):** The brand anchor. Applied to dominant UI containers, major headlines, primary interactive buttons, and navigation rails. Communicates depth, stability, and roasthouse identity.
- **Secondary (`#C77D00` - Warm Saffron Orange):** The vibrant cultural accent. Used selectively for call-to-action highlights, interactive active states, pricing highlights, roast level indicators, and floating checkout triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#F4F7F8` - Soft Light Grey-Blue):** Ambient canvas wash. Replaces stark cool white with an airy, mineral tone reminiscent of polished aggregate and coastal morning light.
- **Neutral (`#1B2428` - High-Contrast Charcoal):** Deep slate-charcoal used for body typography, structural borders, icons, and subtle dividers.

### Theme & Surface System
- **Light Mode (Default):**
  - Canvas: `#F4F7F8`
  - Surface Base: `#FFFFFF` (Pure warm ivory-white for elevated product cards, popovers, and sheets)
  - Surface Alt: `#EBF0F2` (Inset fields, item wells, muted category tracks)
  - Surface Border: `rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.08)`
- **Dark Mode Support:**
  - Canvas: `#0B171B`
  - Surface Base: `#122026`
  - Surface Alt: `#182B33`
  - Surface Border: `rgba(244, 247, 248, 0.12)`
  - Primary Element Fill: `#C77D00`
  - Neutral Text: `#F4F7F8`

## Typography

Headings utilize **Noto Serif**, chosen for its classical editorial authority, sharp serifs, and native harmonic balance with Arabic display scripts (Noto Naskh Arabic pairing). It elevates origin notes, harvest regions, and menu chapters into literary moments. 

Body and interface microcopy utilize **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, comfortable counter spaces, and high legibility across dense ingredient listings and order states.

### Arabic & RTL Considerations
- Set the root document direction to `dir="rtl"` when toggled.
- Headings in Arabic inherit equivalent typographic scale utilizing Noto Naskh Arabic with line-height relaxed by `+0.25rem` to accommodate ascending and descending diacritics.
- Number systems default to localized Western Arabic numerals (`1, 2, 3`) for prices in international café contexts, with fallback support for Eastern Arabic numerals (`١, ٢, ٣`) where configured.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt modular grid structured within a 12-column responsive layout for desktop/tablet, collapsing to a 4-column framework on mobile devices.

### Grid & Form Factor Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width canvas capped at 1280px. Margins set to `margin` (2.5rem), gutters at `gutter` (1.25rem). Asymmetrical editorial splits (7-column hero feature / 5-column daily roast selection).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin set to 2rem, gutters at 1rem. Floating order elements dock to a pinned sticky bottom bar.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Margins set to `margin-mobile` (1rem), gutters at `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem). Interactive menus switch from multi-column grids to vertical cards with horizontal micro-carousels for origin tags and seasonal specials.
- **RTL Reflow:** Grids and flex axes flip horizontally along the inline axis. Margin and padding logic must strictly apply logical CSS properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking** combined with **subtle tinted ambient diffusion**, avoiding synthetic grey drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surface sits directly on `#F4F7F8` without elevation (e.g., standard layout containers, static text panels).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background of `#FFFFFF`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Menu Items):** Shadow: `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.08)`. Surface translates `-2px` along the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Floating Cart Pill & Modals):** Shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.16), 0 0 0 1px rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.06)`. Background uses a high-performance frosted backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Dark Mode Elevation:** Levels are established by increasing surface luminance (`#0B171B` -> `#122026` -> `#182B33`) accented by a hairline border (`rgba(244, 247, 248, 0.1)`), removing heavy drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Containers maintain a crisp, refined architectural stance with subtle corner smoothing that complements the sharp editorial typography:
- Cards, text inputs, modal sheets, and standard containers apply `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`).
- Selectable filter trays and large panels scale up to `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- Circular interactive controls, dietary badge pills, and the floating cart bar utilize continuous full curves (`rounded-full` / pill-shaped) to create immediate ergonomic contrast against structured content boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#12303A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover shifts background to `#1B4250`. Pressed scales to `0.98`.
- **Accent (Order / Reserve):** Background `#C77D00`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover brightens to `#DB8B00`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.24)`, text `#12303A`. On hover, fills with `rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.05)`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Dietary Badges (Organic, Single Origin, Vegan, Gluten-Free):** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, padding `0 10px`. 
  - Standard: Background `#EBF0F2`, text `#12303A`, font token `label-sm`.
  - Highlight / Special Batch: Background `rgba(199, 125, 0, 0.12)`, text `#C77D00`, border `1px solid rgba(199, 125, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, rounded `0.25rem`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.12)`. When selected, fills with `#12303A`, text turns white.

### Lists & Menu Items
- Menu lists feature horizontal item rows with an editorial dotted or hairline baseline separating item name and price.
- Includes a secondary typographic line for tasting notes (e.g., "Bergamot • Dried Fig • Orange Blossom") styled with `body-sm` in `#1B2428` at 70% opacity.
- RTL layout automatically anchors tasting notes to the right, aligning price points flush left.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.16)`, rounded `0.25rem`, internal padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Text is `#1B2428`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#12303A` with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.08)`.
- Checkboxes and radios utilize Deep Teal `#12303A` check fills with crisp contrast markers.

### Cards
- **Product & Origin Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(18, 48, 58, 0.08)`, soft radius `0.25rem`. 
- High-contrast photographic aspect ratio (4:5 or 1:1) with subtle overlay gradients. Contains a top badge slot, title in `Noto Serif` (`headline-sm`), price in bold numeric format, and an inline add-to-order button.

### Floating Cart Pill
- Sticky overlay anchored to the bottom viewport (bottom `1.5rem`), centered horizontally.
- Layout: Full capsule (`rounded-full`), height `52px`, deep teal background `#12303A` with translucent glass sheen (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), text `#FFFFFF`.
- Displays item count indicator in saffron `#C77D00`, price summary in ivory, and a direct checkout arrow (which automatically points left in RTL mode).
